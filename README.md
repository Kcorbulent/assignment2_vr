## VR Assembly
The assembly object we chose to implement is a sandwich. We have one sandwich that is made up of two slices of bread, lunch meat, cheese, lettuce, a tomato, as well as sardines and a candy bar just for the fun of it. We also implemented a second sandwich that includes the same ingredients, but has more of all of them making it a larger sandwich. This second sandwich should meet the requirements for BONUS 1 (15 parts) and BONUS 3 (add two assembly objects)
